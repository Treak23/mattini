   attaches the archives and their checksums to a draft Release that the owner publishes. Not taken:
   (b) a staging branch of built files turned into a Release; (c) the owner builds and uploads by hand;
   (d) keep `dist`. `dist` stays as the rollback record of 0.1.0 to 0.3.1.
2. **0.4.0's delivery → (c):** 0.4.0 is not delivered on its own; the owner gets 0.5.0, which contains
   it. The record of 0.4.0 gets an amendment line: `e2633b6` was lost and never pushed. Not taken: (a)
   0.4.0 as the workflow's first live run; (b) a rebuild pushed to `dist`.
3. **The mask's distance shimmer → (a):** the session decides mipmaps for the capture-shadow mask from a
   distant software render before pinning. Not taken: (b) build without mipmaps as M4-06 pinned.
4. **Lanes → two lanes in this container, written into WORKFLOW §Lanes** as standing practice (Scopes
   disjoint, each gate in its own worktree and `XDG_DATA_HOME`, at most two gates at once).
5. **The go's three lines → as proposed:** builders and fixers on Sonnet; reviewers, provers and
   verifiers on the session's model; two days and about half a week's allowance; two lanes.
