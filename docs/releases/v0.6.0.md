## What you get

- **The far mountains, drawn.** Heights from swissALTIRegio around the village (20 km on every side, 42 x 43 km) are drawn
  as a far terrain at 50 m, coloured from SWISSIMAGE 2023 at 10 m. From the village you now see the Matterhorn, the
  Mischabel and the Weisshorn where they stand, and they cast their shadows on the village: the sun sets behind the real
  ridges instead of the flat horizon (the start date's sun was about 8 minutes early and 24 late until now; 10 to 62
  minutes wrong across the year, by the M4 measurement).
- **Italy's colour is a labelled placeholder.** SWISSIMAGE covers 1220 of the window's 1806 km² cells; where it has no image
  (Italy and the rest of the southern third, 32 % of the far colour) the pixels take the photo's own mean colour for the
  same 100 m of altitude. The pause screen's credits say so ("about 32 % of its pixels are a placeholder, not SWISSIMAGE
  data"). Your choice (a) of 2026-11-03; real colour for Italy is M5.
- **The camera rests on the far terrain.** Outside the 2 km x 3 km area the free-fly camera stays 1.5 m above the far surface
  (found by the review; before the fix it would have flown into the mountainside).
- **A tenth startup line:** "surroundings village loaded, 841 x 861 vertices at 50 m, colour 4200 x 4300 at 10 m"; the
  error panel has an entry for the far terrain with its four commands; the credits carry swissALTIRegio's source text whole
  (TINITALY, geoland.at, Bayern, Baden-Württemberg, IGN) and the Release notes carry the placeholders and the far terrain's
  limits. The camera sees 40 km.
- **Release reproducibility.** The Release build's Python is pinned (3.13.14), a Release is refused from a commit that is
  not on `main`, the release checks say what they found, RELEASE.md's size line speaks.
- **The pipeline:** seven inputs now (swissALTIRegio, SWISSIMAGE at 2 m added), two new steps (`surroundings`, `farcolour`),
  the manifest has twelve keys (328'184 bytes).

## What the review found

Twelve batches (eleven built, one review-fix batch), each reviewed by its tier (tier 1: three lenses and a mutation prover,
a fixer when anything stood and a verifier of its diff; tier 2: one reviewer); the whole-range review over `b4729ab..4ab254a`
with four lenses and two skeptics per finding (`docs/reviews/v0.6.0-round1.md`). **No P0. One P1:** the free-fly camera was
kept above the near terrain only, so flying out of the 2 km x 3 km area (about 40 s at the default speed, 8 s with Shift) put
it inside the far mountainside; found in batch 09's first round, lost with a spend limit, re-found by all four lenses, upheld
by eight skeptics of eight, fixed in v0.6.0-12. **One P2:** a read of a STAC item has no retry (a connection reset aborted
a builder's `lock farphoto` once and `download farphoto` twice through the container's proxy); the dry run and the real run on
GitHub did not meet it, so it stays on the board. The tier-1 provers' first rounds ran 627 mutation rows over ten batches; 126 of them survived the pinned tests
(equivalent mutants included), and the fixers pinned every one that was a real gap; later rounds ran more. They include real
defects the fixers fixed: a malformed zip member name that raised a
traceback instead of a refusal, a digest check that accepted a leading plus or minus (in the far reader only: six others
share it, see the board), and a docstring that promised more than its code did. The coordinates and determinism lens found
no second home for the origin, no 32-bit subtraction, no mirrored placement and nothing in the pipeline's bytes that could
differ by machine or run.

Four record problems, not code: builder agents that ended their turn to wait for a background gate came back BLOCKED with
nothing built (lesson 52, the batch script's rules and BUILD.md now forbid it); a pinned test of 01's fixer round counted the
datasets and broke with the seventh (batch 05's first attempt stopped on it: answered in `docs/history/blocked/

everything that had finished); and the final review report of batch 09 did not carry its camera finding.

## If it will not start

Keep the five files of your platform together in one folder; read `godot.log` (Linux
`~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/mattini/logs/`); if the cause may be MSAA, put an `override.cfg` beside the executable as
the Release notes say; if the window stays blank, wait up to half a minute at the first start; send the log and a screenshot
to the `feedback` session. The rollback: Release v0.5.0 (https://github.com/Treak23/mattini/releases/tag/v0.5.0).
