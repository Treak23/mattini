## What you get

- **The photo's own shadows softened (FB-004 item 3).** The aerial photo was flown on 6 September 2023 with the sun at
  184.9°, so its shadows were painted into the ground; under the moving sun they doubled the real ones. A new pipeline
  step computes where the photo lay in shadow at that moment (a ray cast on swissSURFACE3D at 0.5 m), and the terrain
  lifts the photo inside that mask (G = 3 in linear light, your choice from the comparisons, SPEC §13 rows 41, 47, 55).
  The mask is mipmapped like the photo, so it stays calm in the distance (your "3a": with mipmaps the speckle beyond 350 m
  was 23 to 28 % lower in a software render). A new startup line: "shadows village loaded, 4000 x 6000 cells at 0.5 m".
- **A light haze (FB-004 item 5, variant V2).** Distant forest reads dark blue-grey instead of black; everything far away
  is lighter and bluer (about 10 % at 2 km), coloured by the sky so it follows the sun and darkens at night.
- **The clock's note.** Outside 2020 to 2035, where the sun and moon are validated, the clock label reads "· the sky is
  unchecked outside 2020 to 2035 UTC" and stays on screen; every key still works.
- **Delivery through GitHub Releases (your "1a").** `.github/workflows/release.yml` builds the release on a GitHub runner
  from the pinned inputs, runs the release checks, and attaches a Windows zip, a Linux tar and `SHA256SUMS` to a draft
  Release. No more binaries pushed to `dist`; no 100 MiB limit on a Release asset (the export keeps its own limit until
  M5). `dist` stays as it is, the record of 0.1.0 to 0.3.1.
- **The gate sees more:** a render check that the river's ripples drift the right way (three test streams within half a
  degree, at about 0.7 m/s), that the photo's lift lands on the mask, and that the trees are actually drawn (an empty
  forest used to pass); a cut download says so and that a rerun heals it; the export says "build/ is unfinished, do not
  ship it" when it refuses mid-build; the star table and the sky are guarded against impossible values.
