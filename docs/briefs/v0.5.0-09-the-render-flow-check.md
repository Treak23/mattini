## Goal

The gate's render step fails when the river's ripples drift upstream, across their bed or not at
all. A fourth render script, `game/tests/render/render_flow.gd` (pinned, byte for byte), draws the
complete test world flat, every class texel water, with a flow texture of three bands (east, north,
south-west) on the flow grid's own 2 m cells, takes two frames 15 drawn frames apart under
`--fixed-fps 60` (0.25 s of shader TIME, brain.md H29), finds each band's ripple pattern again by
cross-correlation, and prints one line, `RENDER flow: PASS|FAIL …`, naming per band the measured
direction against the band's flow and the speed. `tools/gate.sh` runs it with the others, every
render script at `--fixed-fps 60`, and its SKIPPED line and header say so. Nothing the owner sees
changes.

Production code is `main` at the head you start from (rehearsed on the tree of briefs 01 to 06's
and 10's rehearsals: `main` at `d522abf` with their pins and throwaways, head `2271db5`). The pin
author rehearsed this brief in a clone of it (2026-10-30): the pins red, green on a throwaway
`gate.sh` edit, the full gate, every mutation row, and the check under software Forward+. Every
number marked "rehearsal" was measured there. The session then re-cut the pins on `origin/main` at
`b253cdd` (briefs 01 to 06 landed), changed `render_flow.gd` so that its flow also varies by row

1. **The check, `render_flow.gd`** (session: from v0.4.0-03's probe; pin author: its shape, the
   pinned file). A SceneTree script like `render_lift.gd`: the complete world (`complete_world.gd`)
   with one flat level of terrain, built by `TerrainLoader.create(grid, scene, photo, surfaces)`
   (four arguments: the no-mask texel, nothing lifted); the photo replaced by one grey of 0.6 and
   `outside_albedo` set to the same grey (no static edge in the windows), every class texel water
   (2), and `water_flow` replaced by a texture of the flow grid's own size (20 × 15 cells of 2 m on
   the SurfaceGrid's rectangle, as the loader places it): columns 0 to 3 flow east (bearing 90°),
   4 to 7 north (0°), 8 to 19 south-west (225°), each encoded as SurfaceGrid encodes a bearing
   (R = round(128 + 127 x), G = round(128 + 127 z), B = 255: (255, 128, 255), (128, 1, 255),
   (38, 218, 255)). The bands hold in the flow grid's first 7 rows (from the north: local z 9 to
   23, under every window); rows 7 to 14 hold each band's reverse bearing (east's columns flow west,
   and so on), so a flow read mirrored north-south lands the windows on rows 8 to 13 and every band
   reads about 180° off (mutation row 20). Rows 7 to 14 lie south of the terrain (it ends at z 22):
   the two frames are byte for byte those of a flow whose every row is the same (re-cut, measured).
   One light 20° above the horizon from the bearing 30° (light energy 4.0, no highlight; the water
   renders about 160 of 255, its ripples spread about 6), no ambient light, a linear tone map, MSAA
   off, the camera 9 m above (1025, 614, 16.25), tilted 89° (nearly straight down). Two frames 15 drawn frames apart; the check fails unless every process step
   between them was exactly 1/60 s and exactly 15 frames were drawn. In each band's window (local
   x 1015.6 to 1019.6, 1022.4 to 1027.6 and 1030.4 to 1034.4, z 11.1 to 21.4; each between the
   centres of its band's first and last flow column and of the first and last band row, checked), every second pixel's luminance in
   the first frame is matched in the second: the whole-pixel shift up to 16 px each way that
   maximises the normalised cross-correlation, refined by a parabola, turned into metres through
   the camera's rays. **PASS** when, for every band, the shift's direction is within 5° of the
   band's flow (decoded from the bytes handed to the shader, `((R, G) · 255 − 128) / 127 · B`, at
   the band's first column in row 0),
   its speed is 0.5 to 1.0 m/s (the shader's two maps drift at 0.8 and 0.5 m/s), its correlation
   is at least 0.6, its window holds at least 5000 samples and the first frame's luminance there
   spreads at least 2.0 (a pattern to follow). Rehearsal (every run the same line;
   the two frames byte-identical run to run; re-cut at `b253cdd`, the same line digit for digit):
   ```
   RENDER flow: PASS the ripples' shift in 0.25 s (15 frames at 60 fps) against each band's flow: east +0.2° off at 0.71 m/s (shift +0.178 m east +0.000 m south, correlation 0.872, unmoved 0.359, 27950 samples), north +0.2° off at 0.77 m/s (shift +0.001 m east -0.193 m south, correlation 0.797, unmoved 0.121, 36335 samples), south-west +0.4° off at 0.72 m/s (shift -0.129 m east +0.127 m south, correlation 0.757, unmoved 0.013, 27951 samples); allowed 5° off and 0.50 to 1.00 m/s
   ```
   The thresholds' margins at the rehearsal's head (lesson 45): the angles 0.2 to 0.4° (allowed
   5°), the speeds 0.71 to 0.77 m/s (allowed 0.5 to 1.0), the correlations 0.757 to 0.872 (floor
   0.6), the spreads 5.74 to 6.52 (floor 2.0). Software Forward+ prints the same to 0.001 m. Why 0.25 s (pin author): at 0.10 s (6 frames) the correlations are higher (0.924 to 0.958)
   but the 4-pixel shift reads the south-west band 3.9° off; at 0.25 s the 10-pixel shift reads
   every band within 0.5°. At 0.5 s the direction is ambiguous by construction (v0.4.0-03's
   probe: the two phases are half a period apart). A number that differs at your head is recorded,
   never tuned (card 4).
