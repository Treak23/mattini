## What 0.4.0 delivers

- **The river flows downstream (FB-008):** every stream in the village flows along its own course, the direction
  taken from swissTLM3D's watercourse lines, which swisstopo draws downstream (SPEC §13 row 53): a 2 m flow grid
  from the surfaces step, read by the game through the world config, both ripple maps drifting along it in two
  crossfaded phases whose period divides an hour (0.3.1's hourly jump, R1-03, is gone).
- **The moving sun (FB-004 items 1 and 7):** the sun and the moon where they stand over Zermatt at the game's time,
  from the photo's moment (6 September 2023, 13:40 CEST); the physical sky with twilight and the sun's disk;
  exposure 0.70 by day, 4.0 at night; the sky's ground in the valley's own colour so shaded walls and tree sides
  keep 0.3.1's light (row 52); the stars from the Hipparcos Catalogue, credited to ESA (row 51); the moon by its
  phase; the keys `,` `.` (∓15 min), `[` `]` (∓1 day), `T` (1×, 60×, 600×, stopped), `N` (now) and a clock label.
- **Four leftovers (the owner's "4 all four"):** a pipeline file error prints one line (I-03, in 07); the release
  checklist's frame-cap and `override.cfg` checks (words, in this commit); the clerk's commits chained to the
  hygiene tests (words, in this commit); the release-hints census without its `until_m4_10` exemption (F6, in 09).
- **FB-004 item 5 (the trees):** no build; at the release gate the session renders three views in software Forward+
  with three variants (crowns brightened, a haze, a brighter sky ground from 2.6 × K) for the owner to choose from.
