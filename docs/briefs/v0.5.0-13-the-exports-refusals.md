for the pack tool packs an empty folder into an empty pack, where `game/tools/pack.gd` refuses it,

export, and nothing says so (R1-06 b). And 22 of 31 mutations of the script's refusals left its

on ends with the line `export: build/ is unfinished, do not ship it`, and one before step 4 does
not; pinned tests fail step 5 on a file over 100 MiB and step 4c on a generated file missing from
