## Goal

The far terrain's two steps become commands, their outputs reach the game through the manifest,
and the rules they share have one home each:

- `python -m mattini surroundings` builds the 50 m heights (v0.6.0-04's `build_surroundings`)
  and `python -m mattini farcolour` the 10 m colour (v0.6.0-06's `build_farcolour`) from the
  downloads, as `shadows` builds the mask; each then writes the manifest and prints its path.
- The manifest gains one key, `surroundings` (twelve keys at the top): the heights' description
  and the colour's side by side, 20 keys, the shape v0.6.0-08's reader reads; each half checked
  world, then the lock's fit, then the inputs, then the half itself; null until both are built
  (the heights alone give null); the colour alone is refused, naming `python -m mattini
  surroundings` (decision 4).
- The swissALTIRegio tile rule and the SWISSIMAGE 2 m cell rule are asked in one place,
  `lock.fit_problem`, with the surroundings config it is given; `config/surroundings.json` is read
  by one function, `surroundings.load_surroundings_config`. v0.6.0-01's small reader in
  `download.py` and v0.6.0-04's and -06's own fit code go.

The eight earlier fixture manifests gain one line, `"surroundings": null`. Nothing in the game
changes (v0.6.0-08 reads the block; v0.6.0-09 draws it).

1. **The commands** (M4-09 decision 1, verbatim for the surroundings; `farcolour` added: design

     `data/raw`) and `--out` (default `game/assets/generated`), "as `trees` does", with the other
     steps' help texts for the options. Each loads `config/world.json`, `datasets.json`,
     `datasets.lock.json` and `config/surroundings.json` (both steps read the one file, design

     stdout (progress on stderr):
     ```python
     surroundings.build_surroundings(world, datasets, pinned, surroundings.load_surroundings_config(), raw_root, out_dir, progress=progress)
     farcolour.build_farcolour(world, datasets, pinned, surroundings.load_surroundings_config(), raw_root, out_dir, progress=progress)
     return write_manifest(out_dir)
     ```

     `surroundings.load_surroundings_config` on their modules: a name imported into `__main__`
     would not be replaced.

     roofs, surfaces, trees, surroundings, farcolour, shadows, sky, manifest` (M4-09's order with
     `farcolour` after `surroundings`). The rehearsal put both in `STEPS` before `shadows` and
     gave `_build_step` two branches.
