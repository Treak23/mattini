## Goal

The owner moves the game's time with six keys: `,` and `.` move it 15 minutes earlier or later,
`[` and `]` a day, `T` steps the speed 1×, 60×, 600×, stopped, and `N` sets the computer's clock. A
label at the bottom left says the time and the speed (`6 Sep 2023 13:40 CEST · 1×`) for 4 s after a
key, and always while the pause screen is open; the pause screen lists the six keys. The sun, the
moon and the stars follow the clock (v0.4.0-08 already places them every frame); the river's
ripples keep running in real time. An export refuses a manifest without the sky block, naming
`python -m mattini sky`. The release-hints census covers every reader, the far mountains' and the
capture mask's included from their first commit.

Production code is `main` after v0.4.0-08. The pin author rehearsed this brief in a clone of the
session's `main` with v0.4.0-01 to -08's pins and throwaway implementations merged in
(2026-10-27): the pins red on that code, green on a throwaway implementation, the full gate with
its render step, and twenty-four mutation rows and two pin-side checks both ways. Every number
marked "rehearsal" was measured there.
The pin author's record is `docs/briefs/sketches/v0.4.0/v0.4.0-09-PINSPEC.md`, committed by the
session; you do not need it.
