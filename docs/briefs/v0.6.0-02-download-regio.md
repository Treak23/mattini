## Goal

`python -m mattini download regio` fetches and verifies the 30 swissALTIRegio tiles the
village's surroundings need into `data/raw/swissaltiregio/2026-06/`, with a provenance.json,
exactly as `download terrain` fetches swissALTI3D (SPEC §5): the lock first (it must fit the
configs, the tiles the surroundings config asks for), every STAC item compared with the lock
before any file is fetched, a republished tile refused naming `lock regio` and design's step
for `config/surroundings.json`, the files through `.part`, verified, one retry. v0.6.0-01 wrote
the lock entry; this brief is the first that writes the tiles to disk.

No step reads them yet (the surroundings step is v0.6.0-04, lane B, with its own fixture); no
manifest, nothing in the game, no change to the lock.

## Decisions already made

Decisions 1 to 3 are brief M4-01's decision 6 (design, 2026-10-17; its data stand, design §0),

1. **`download regio`** (`--out`, default data/raw) follows the terrain's flow (M4-01 decision
   6, verbatim but for the marked words):
   - first `lock.fit_problem` (collection, edition, resolution, exactly the tiles
     `regio_tiles` gives), naming `python -m mattini lock regio` and no other command, before
     swisstopo is asked anything;
   - then every item: its datetime in the edition, its published name and SHA-256 the lock's,
     all before any request for a file and before anything on disk changes;
   - the republish refusal names the tile, both checksums, "nothing was fetched",
     `python -m mattini lock regio`, "then commit the lock" and design's step for
     **config/surroundings.json**;
   - then the files: `.part`, verified, renamed, one retry, a verified file kept;
   - `swissaltiregio/2026-06/provenance.json` gets exactly the terrain's keys (`attribution`
     the whole source text): {attribution, collection, dataset, edition, resolution_m,
     schema: 1, tiles: [{file, item, sha256, size_bytes, tile, url}] in the lock's tile order},
     sorted keys, 2-space indent, UTF-8, LF, a final newline, no times, no paths;
   - `fit_problem` gains the `swissaltiregio` row. **0.6.0, the session's decision** (M4-01
     left "a keyword argument, or the reader" to the builder; the pins call `fit_problem` with
     the five arguments every other dataset takes): the row's tiles are
     `download.regio_tiles(world, area, margin_m=…, spacing_m=…)` with the two numbers
     `download.surroundings_settings(area)` returns **when the fit is asked** (v0.6.0-01
     decision 3: it reads `download.SURROUNDINGS_PATH` at call time; the pins patch that
     attribute). Keys compared: collection, edition, resolution_m. A config the reader refuses
     is its `DownloadError`, raised out of `fit_problem` like `dataset_of`'s: `download regio`
     ends in one `mattini: error:` line naming the file, before any request, with nothing on
     disk. The fit follows both numbers (the session's ruling of 2026-11-02): at the margin of
     20'000 every spacing from 1 to 2'000 m gives the same 30 tiles (the east edge lies on a
     tile edge), so the pin `test_the_fit_follows_the_config_s_spacing` sets `spacing_m` 4'000
     with the margin unchanged (measured: 36 tiles, the row N 1064 added) and expects the
     committed lock refused naming `2595-1064` and `2645-1064`.

3. **The cut transfer** (0.6.0: brief v0.5.0-10, `78853de`, landed after M4-01 cut its pins).
   A body cut before its Content-Length is a fetch error of its own (`cut at <n> of <size>
   bytes`), retried once; cut twice, the refusal names the tile, the URL, `(after 2 attempts)`
   and `; a dropped connection heals on a rerun: run the command again`; a whole body with
   another SHA-256 twice ends `does not match the published … (after 2 attempts)` with no
   rerun advice. `download regio` gets this from the shared `_fetch_verified`, unchanged: no

   fetcher bound at import time fails it although it behaves the same.
