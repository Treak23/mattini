## Goal

SWISSIMAGE's 2 m asset of every 1 km cell around the village is a pinned input like every
other (SPEC §5): `config/datasets.json` names the dataset `swissimage_far`, `python -m mattini
lock farphoto` writes the lock's `swissimage_far` entry from the STAC items alone (the 1220
cells of the surroundings box that have an item of edition 2023, with each file's SHA-256, and
the 586 that have none), and `python -m mattini download farphoto` fetches and verifies the
1220 files into `data/raw/swissimage_far/2023/` with a provenance.json, refusing before any
fetch a tile swisstopo republished, an item that appeared in a cell the lock records as absent,
and an item that vanished. The cells come from the area's box and the `margin_m` that
`config/surroundings.json` gives (design §2: the box itself, no half-spacing border).

No step reads the files yet (the far colour step is v0.6.0-06, lane B); no manifest, nothing in
the game, no credit line (design §2: the photo's line already names SWISSIMAGE 2023).
