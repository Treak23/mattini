## What 0.6.0 delivers

- **The far mountains drawn (SPEC §13 row 59):** swissALTIRegio's heights for 20 km around the village as a drawn
  far terrain at 50 m (the exact-centimetre mean of 25 samples), coloured from SWISSIMAGE 2023's 2 m tiles at 10 m
  where Swiss and filled by altitude elsewhere (a labelled placeholder; the owner's question below). It casts the
  mountains' shadows on the village (the sun sets behind the real ridges) and shows the Matterhorn, the Mischabel
  and the Weisshorn from the village. The camera sees 40 km. A tenth startup line; an error-panel entry; the
  credits gain swissALTIRegio's whole source text.
- **Release reproducibility:** the Release build's Python pinned (3.13.14, the 0.5.0 Release's), a Release refused
  from a sha not on `main`, the release checks' check 5 telling the truth, RELEASE.md §C's size line speaking.
- **The version 0.6.0;** the Release notes' rollback becomes Release v0.5.0.
