## Goal

`pipeline/mattini/farcolour.py` turns SWISSIMAGE's 2 m tiles into the colour of the far terrain
that brief v0.6.0-08 lays over the 50 m mesh (design §2, §3):

- **The grid:** a pixel every 10 m over the surroundings box (the area's box grown by 20 km):
  4200 × 4300 for the village.
- **A covered pixel** is the integer mean of its 5 × 5 source pixels.
- **A pixel of a cell with no SWISSIMAGE item** (Italy, a third of the box) is a labelled
  placeholder (Hard Rule 2): the mean colour of the covered pixels at the same altitude (its 100 m
  height band, from swissALTIRegio), so the far side takes the photo's own tones by altitude.
- **The output:** a JPEG made exactly as the photo's. Its pixels and bytes are compared with
  design's values before anything is written, and the share of filled pixels is recorded.

The command line and the manifest come in brief v0.6.0-07. This brief builds the step and the
check the manifest will call, not their wiring.

2. **A covered pixel** (design §2): a pixel of a 1 km cell the lock pins a tile for is
   `(sum + 12) // 25` per channel of its 5 × 5 source pixels, counted from the cell's north-west
   corner (the tiles' pixel edges lie on the km grid, design §0). Exact integer arithmetic.
3. **A filled pixel** (design §2; the session's reading where marked):
   - every pixel of a cell the lock records as `absent` is filled;
   - its band is `floor(h / band_m)`, h the swissALTIRegio sample at the pixel's centre in whole
     centimetres (`cents // (100 · band_m)`, floor also below 0), the height as swisstopo gives it
     (**not** minus `height_offset_m`);
   - its colour is the **half-up integer mean** per channel, `(total + n // 2) // n`, of every
     covered pixel of that band (the session's reading of design's "integer mean": the same
     rounding as `(sum + 12) // 25`; the means of the covered 10 m pixels, not of their source
     pixels); the band totals and counts are accumulated in 64-bit integers (the fixture's
     totals are small, band 21's red 16950, so a uint16 total passes every pin and goes wrong
     only on the real data: the checker measured it);
   - a band with no covered pixel takes the band mean of the nearest band **below** that has one;
     only if none below has one, the nearest above (design §2; so a band equally near one below
     and one above takes the one below);
   - every pixel centre (covered or not) must have its sample; one without is refused naming the
     pixel's row, column, LV95 position and `python -m mattini download regio`.
   - No pixel is covered: refused (no colour to fill with).
   - Measured on the real data (rehearsal): every band that holds a filled pixel (8 to 46, heights
     850.37 to 4634.17 m) also holds covered pixels, so the nearest-band rule does not act on the
     village today; the fixture pins it.
