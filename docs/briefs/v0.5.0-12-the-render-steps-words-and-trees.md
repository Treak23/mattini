## Goal

The gate's render step says what it compares and fails where it was blind. Its words name the
water class, the one class it reads, never "the surface classes" (R1-05, R2-P-03; lesson 44). The
classes check decides a shifted water class by how far the agreement at no shift lies below the
best of its scan, against a measured margin per axis, and prints both gaps, instead of an argmax
that clean runs win by 6 samples (R2-P-04). The trees check renders its view a second time with
the Trees node hidden and fails unless every crown's disc differs from the bare ground, printing
`20 of 20 crowns drawn`: today it passes with no tree drawn (R2-P-01). Nothing the owner sees
changes.

Production code is `main` at the head you start from. The pin author rehearsed this brief first on
a rehearsal tree (head `2d8d982`, briefs 01 to 06's, 10's and 09's rehearsals); the pins were then
re-cut on `origin/main` at `0aecfa0` (briefs 01 to 07 and 09 landed) and rehearsed again there
(2026-10-30): the pins red, green on a throwaway `gate.sh` edit, the full gate, every mutation row.
Every number marked "rehearsal" was measured on `0aecfa0` (the RENDER lines are the rehearsal
tree's digit for digit). The pin author's record is `docs/briefs/sketches/v0.5.0/v0.5.0-12-PINSPEC.md`, committed by
the session; you do not need it.
