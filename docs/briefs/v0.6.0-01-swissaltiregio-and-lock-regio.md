## Goal

swissALTIRegio, swisstopo's 10 m terrain model of Switzerland and its neighbours (10 km tiles of
zipped XYZ text), is a pinned input like every other (SPEC §5): `config/datasets.json` names it
with the source text its terms require, and `python -m mattini lock regio` writes the lock's
`swissaltiregio` entry, the SHA-256 of the 30 tiles the village's surroundings need, from the
STAC items alone. The tiles come from the area's box, the `margin_m` and the `spacing_m` that
`config/surroundings.json` gives (design §1: 50 m since 0.6.0; still the 30 tiles M4-01
measured). The committed lock then holds the entry that `config/surroundings.json` records as
`computed_for.inputs_sha256`: design's expected heights and the inputs meet here.

No download, no step, no manifest, nothing in the game: `download regio` is v0.6.0-02, the
surroundings step v0.6.0-04.

## Decisions already made

Decisions 1 to 6 are brief M4-01's decisions 1 to 5, 7 and 8 (design, 2026-10-17; their data
stand, design §0), quoted with only what 0.6.0 changed marked "0.6.0"; "the session's decision"

1. **The dataset** (M4-01 decision 1, the pinned `config/datasets.json`, byte-identical to
   M4-01's pinned copy; the live file has not changed since M4-01 cut it on `d1dc170`).
   - `swissaltiregio`: collection `ch.swisstopo.swissaltiregio`, edition `2026-06`,
     `resolution_m` 10 (an integer).
   - `attribution`: the product page's source text for the 2025 and later versions, verbatim
     as design corrected it (m4-design §1a), one line. The credits print it whole.
   - `resolution_m` is required for it, as for the other datasets read at a resolution
     (`datasets.RESOLUTION_DATASETS`).
2. **What the live STAC API publishes** (M4-01 decision 2, measured 2026-10-17, re-measured by
   design on 2026-11-02, design §0: the same 30 checksums, the same edition):
   - one national item `swissaltiregio` (a 10.1 GB COG; not used: its bytes cannot be verified
     in part) and 2'365 tile items `swissaltiregio_<E km>-<N km>`;
   - the id names the tile's **south-west corner** in whole kilometres, four digits each
     (`2615-1084`, `2385-0974`); the corners lie at E = 5 km + 10 km·i and N = 4 km + 10 km·j;
   - each tile item has exactly one asset, `swissaltiregio_<tile>_2056_5728.xyz.zip`
     (`application/x.ascii-xyz+zip`), with `file:checksum` `1220` + SHA-256 in upper-case hex;
   - **the ids carry no edition.** Every tile item's `properties.datetime` is
     `2026-06-01T00:00:00Z`; hence the edition `2026-06`.
3. **The tiles of an area** (M4-01 decision 4; the spacing changed by 0.6.0's design §1):
   - the surroundings box is the area's box grown by `margin_m`;
   - a vertex every `spacing_m` takes the samples in the half-open square
     [E − s/2, E + s/2) × [N − s/2, N + s/2), so the samples needed lie in
     [e_min − s/2, e_max + s/2) × [n_min − s/2, n_max + s/2) of that box;
   - `download.regio_tiles(world, area, *, margin_m, spacing_m)` lists every tile whose square
     [E0, E0 + 10 km) × [N0, N0 + 10 km) meets that region: east outer, north inner, sorted;
   - `margin_m` and `spacing_m` come from the area's entry in `config/surroundings.json`
     (**0.6.0: 20'000 and 50** for the village, design §1 and §5; M4-01 had 100); the area is
     the one `config/terrain.json` names, as for every other input;
   - the village needs 6 × 5 = **30 tiles**, E 2595 … 2645, N 1074 … 1114, at 50 m as at 100 m
     (rehearsal: the region [2'602'975, 2'645'025) × [1'074'975, 1'118'025) at 50 m). Its east
     edge (2'645'000) lies on a tile edge: the column 2645 is needed only for the half square
     beyond it (samples E 2'645'005 and 2'645'015 at 50 m);
   - **the reader** (M4-01: "a small reader in `download.py`, two whole numbers for the area,
     refused naming the file otherwise"; the session's decision makes its name and its patch
     point exact, since the pins call it): `download.surroundings_settings(area, path=None)`
     returns `(margin_m, spacing_m)`; with `path` None it reads `download.SURROUNDINGS_PATH`
     (`REPO / "config" / "surroundings.json"`) **when it is called**, not when the module loads
     (the pins patch the module attribute). Each value a JSON integer (not a float, a text or
     a boolean), `margin_m` ≥ 0, `spacing_m` ≥ 1. A file that cannot be read or is not JSON, a
     missing area, or any other value is a `DownloadError` whose text names the file and the
     area or key at fault. It reads nothing else of the file (no schema check: the step's own
     loader is v0.6.0-04's; v0.6.0-07 may move this reader onto it).
4. **The lock entry** `swissaltiregio` (M4-01 decision 5), exact keys:
   - {collection, edition, resolution_m, tiles: {<tile>: {file, sha256}}}, the checksum in
     lower case;
   - written only by `python -m mattini lock regio`, from the STAC items only: no request to
     the file host, not even a HEAD;
   - it replaces its own entry and keeps every other;
   - its `collection`, `edition` and `resolution_m` are read from `config/datasets.json` when
     the command runs, never written as constants (the session's ruling of 2026-11-02, on the
     checker's finding): with the one committed config a constant writes the same bytes, so the
     pin patches the config (edition `2026-07`, `resolution_m` 20, the items dated
     `2026-07-01`) and expects an entry carrying `2026-07` and 20. The pin replaces
     `load_datasets` as `mattini.__main__` imports it on `main` (`from mattini.datasets import
     load_datasets`); keep that import;
   - before writing, every item's `properties.datetime` must lie in the pinned edition (it
     starts with `<edition>-`); else it is refused naming the tile, its datetime and the
     edition, and nothing is written. Without this a new edition would be locked under the old
     name. A tile with no item is refused, "cannot read the STAC item";
   - the reader of decision 3 runs before swisstopo is asked anything: a config it refuses
     ends `lock regio` in one `mattini: error:` line naming the file, with no request and the
     lock unchanged;
   - `lock.validate_lock` knows the entry, and the malformed-lock error names all **six** lock
     commands (`lock.LOCK_COMMANDS` gains `python -m mattini lock regio`; it is also
     `download.LOCK_COMMAND_OF["swissaltiregio"]`).
