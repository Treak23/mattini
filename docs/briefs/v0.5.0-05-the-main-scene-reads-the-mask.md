## Goal

The game the owner starts lifts the aerial photo's baked shadows (SPEC §13 rows 41 and 47): the
main scene reads the capture-shadow mask (`CaptureShadowGrid`, v0.5.0-03) after the roof colours
and hands it to `TerrainLoader.create` as its fifth argument (v0.5.0-04), so the photo shader lifts
the photo where it lay in shadow when it was flown. A missing or refused mask opens the error panel
with "the photo's softened shadows" and its two commands, and everything else is still shown, the
photo unlifted. The started game prints one more startup line after the roofs',
`mattini <v>: shadows village loaded, 4000 x 6000 cells at 0.5 m` for the real village, and the
credits carry the mask's dataset line (the trees' `swisssurface3d 2021-2022, © swisstopo`, listed
once). `tools/export.sh` requires the shadows block and that line. The gate's photo check keeps its
numbers: `render_photo.gd` switches the lift off in its views.

Production code is `main` at the head you start from (rehearsed on the merge of lane G's and lane
P's rehearsals: `main` at `d522abf` with v0.5.0-01 to -04's pins and throwaways). The pin author
rehearsed this brief in a clone of it (2026-10-30): the pins red, green on a throwaway, the full
gate, all 17 mutation rows, and the photo check under software Forward+. Every number marked
"rehearsal" was measured there. The pin author's record is
`docs/briefs/sketches/v0.5.0/v0.5.0-05-PINSPEC.md`, committed by the session; you do not need it.
