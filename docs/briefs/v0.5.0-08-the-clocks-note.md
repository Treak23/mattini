## Goal

The sun, the moon and the stars were checked against the vectors in `tests/shared/sky_vectors.json`,
whose 295 instants lie between 27 Jan 2020 and 24 Dec 2035 (UTC); the day keys, 600x and the
computer's clock (N) can take the game's clock anywhere, and nothing on the screen says the sky there
is unchecked. When this is done, `SkyClock` holds the validated range as two named constants and a
check, and while the game's clock is outside that range the clock label says so in plain words and
stays shown until the clock is back inside. The keys keep working: nothing is clamped. Inside the
range the label is exactly what it was.

Production code is `main` at the head you start from. The pin author rehearsed this brief first on
the integration rehearsal's tree (`5a1751f`), then re-cut and rehearsed it again on `main` at
`d4f301e` (2026-10-30; v0.5.0-01 to -07 and -09 landed, its `main.gd` v0.5.0-05's real change, which
changed nothing near the clock; `sky_clock.gd` as at `d522abf`): the pin red, green on a throwaway,
the full gate, all 17 mutation rows and the three label frames. Every number marked "rehearsal" was
measured on `d4f301e`. The pin author's record is
`docs/briefs/sketches/v0.5.0/v0.5.0-08-PINSPEC.md`; you need from it only the scripts named below.

## Decisions already made

"(pin author; accepted by the session 2026-10-30)" marks a choice the pin author made while
rehearsing.

1. **The note, not a clamp** (the owner's "question A OK", 2026-10-29, on the session's list: "the
   clock's note outside the validated 2020 to 2035 (the keys keep working)"; the triage's item
   T-clock, option "Label outside the range"). Every key moves the clock by its full step on both
   sides of each bound; the clock runs on past them at every speed; N still reads the computer's
   clock. No clamp anywhere.
2. **The range lives in `SkyClock` only** (Hard Rule 4's spirit: one home), beside `START_UNIX`,
   as floats in Unix seconds UTC with a doc comment:
   - `const VALID_FROM_UNIX := 1577836800.0` (2020-01-01T00:00:00Z);
   - `const VALID_TO_UNIX := 2082758400.0` (2036-01-01T00:00:00Z);
   - `static func in_validated_range(unix: float) -> bool`: true from `VALID_FROM_UNIX` inclusive up
     to `VALID_TO_UNIX` exclusive; false for NaN and both infinities (write it so a NaN fails both
     comparisons, `unix >= VALID_FROM_UNIX and unix < VALID_TO_UNIX`, not as `not (outside)`).
   The bounds round the vectors' first and last instants out to whole years: the first vector is
   27.0 days after `VALID_FROM_UNIX`, the last 7.1 days before `VALID_TO_UNIX` (rehearsal, from the
   file; the pin holds both within 31 days). `main.gd` asks `SkyClock.in_validated_range` and holds
   neither bound as a number.
3. **The words, exactly** (pin author; accepted by the session 2026-10-30): outside the range
   `clock_text()` returns its usual text (at every speed, `stopped` too) followed by
   ` · the sky is unchecked outside 2020 to 2035 UTC` (a space, U+00B7 MIDDLE DOT, a space, then
   the words), e.g. `1 Jan 2036 13:00 CET · 1× · the sky is unchecked outside 2020 to 2035 UTC`.
   Keep the words in one named constant in `main.gd` beside `CLOCK_SHOWN_S` (the throwaway called it
   `CLOCK_NOTE`; the name is not pinned). "UTC" because the bounds are UTC instants and the label
   reads Zurich's time: from 00:00 to 00:59 CET on 1 Jan 2020 the label reads 2020 and is outside;
   from 00:00 to 00:59 CET on 1 Jan 2036 it reads 2036 and is inside. The words stay true there.
4. **When the label shows** (the session's design, accepted 2026-10-30; the go says only "the
   clock's note ... (the keys keep working)"):
   - inside the range, exactly as v0.4.0-09 pinned it: hidden at the start, shown `CLOCK_SHOWN_S`
     real seconds after a time key, always while the pause screen is open;
   - outside it, shown at every frame of `_process`, however the clock got there (a key, the clock
     running, `time_unix` set directly by a test or a probe), and never hidden by the countdown; the
     check reads the time after that frame's step, so a clock that runs past a bound is shown in the
     same frame;
   - back inside, it hides `CLOCK_SHOWN_S` after the last key (or at the next frame when no key
     brought it back), as before. Closing the pause screen outside may hide it until the next frame.
   While shown, every frame writes `clock_text()` on it, as v0.4.0-09 made it.
5. **The comments say what the code now does** (Hard Rule 2): `sky_clock.gd`'s class comment no
   longer says only "nothing is clamped": it names `VALID_FROM_UNIX`, `VALID_TO_UNIX` and
   `in_validated_range` and says the game's clock label shows the note outside the range (the
   pin reads that the three names appear before `const START_UNIX`). `main.gd`'s class comment
   (the paragraph on the time keys and UI/Clock) names `in_validated_range` and says when the label
   stays shown; `_process`'s and `clock_text`'s doc comments say what they now do.
6. **A new test file, not `test_m4_10_clock.gd` re-pinned** (pin author; accepted by the session
   2026-10-30): v0.4.0-09's pinned copy stays byte-identical, and v0.5.0-11 (after you, on
   `sky_clock.gd`) is free to re-pin the clock's own tests without touching yours.
7. **The evidence frames** (not a gate): the pin author's script
   `docs/briefs/sketches/v0.5.0/v0.5.0-08-label.gd` renders `main.tscn` on the game tests' complete
   test world (no village needed) under Compatibility, three frames: `inside` (15 minutes after the
   start, by `time_later`), `outside` (`time_unix` set to 2036-01-01T12:00:00Z, 5 s of frames, no
   key), `paused` (the same, the pause screen open). Saved as JPEG quality 90 to
   `docs/briefs/shots/v0.5.0-08-label-<inside|outside|paused>.jpg`. Rehearsal: the label's text

   2020 to 2035 UTC` twice, shown each time; the note ends about 606 px from the left of a
   1280 px frame, below Resume and Quit.
8. **Not in this batch:** a clamp; the controls list's words; `SkyClock`'s formulas and their
