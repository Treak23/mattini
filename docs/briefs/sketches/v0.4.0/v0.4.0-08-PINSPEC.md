## Where this departs from the session's decisions or M4-10, and why

1. **No sky without a world config** (M4-10 decision 3: "Without a world config the owner's axes
   stand in (so the placeholder has a direction; nothing else is built then)"). The owner's axes
   written into `main.gd` would be a second home for the axis mapping (Hard Rule 4), and exactly the
   shape of M4-10's own mutation "the owner's axes hard-coded in `_direction`". With nothing built,
   the sun has nothing to light; measured: no ERROR line in either renderer, the clear colour
   behind the panel. Reversible in one function (`_create_sky` with the owner's frame); the
   session's call (question 1).
2. **The light pin is extended, not reused as pinned** (the triage: "reuse as pinned: sky_site,
   light"). The session's decision 1 puts "`time_unix` at 1x" and "`_place_sky` every frame" in this
   batch, and M4-10 pinned them only in its clock pin (v0.4.0-09's): without a test here, a builder
   who never calls `_place_sky` from `_process`, or runs the clock while paused, passes every pin
   (rows 17 and 18 now fail one test each). The second added test checks the complete world's `SKY`
   against `config/sky.json` (lesson 42: the start test's 184.65/50.34 depends on it).
3. **The pause stops the clock in this batch** (M4-10 decision 2, "unless the pause screen is
   open", and m4-design §9 "Pause: time stops", brought forward from v0.4.0-09's half): `_process`
   is born here, and a clock that runs behind the pause screen for one batch is a behaviour the
   owner would see. v0.4.0-09 keeps it and multiplies by its speeds.
4. **`test_main_scene.gd` and the allowlist line are pinned** (M4-10 decision 10 and 11 made them
   the builder's own edits, with the exact words): pinned, so the `cmp` gate holds their exact text.
5. **The SkySite pin**: its header names this brief and v0.4.0-07; one dead helper (`_remove_tree`,
   never called) removed. Its ten tests are M4-10's, unchanged.
6. **STARS_NOTE is tied to the table** (a new assertion beside M4-10's): the credit must equal the
