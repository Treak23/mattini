## The motion probe (evidence, not a gate; the session's decision 6)

Measured fact (Godot 4.6.3 sources, `main/main.cpp` lines 4841 and 4990/4994): the shader's TIME
advances by `process_step * time_scale` per drawn frame, and with `--fixed-fps N` the process step
is exactly 1/N. So under `--fixed-fps 60` shader TIME is the drawn frames / 60, whatever the
software renderer's speed, and two runs gave byte-identical frames (SHA-256 of all four PNGs equal).
(The triage's open question "whether Engine.time_scale controls shader TIME": it does, through the
step; and `--fixed-fps` makes it deterministic.)

`evidence/probe_flow.gd` (a SceneTree script outside the repository): the complete test world flat
(as `render_photo.gd` builds it), MSAA off, camera at (1025, 614, 16.25) looking straight down; the
photo a uniform grey texel over the whole view; every class texel water (110 × 68 at 0.2 m on
(1014, 9.5, 22, 13.5)); a 33 × 1 RGB8 flow on the same rectangle, three bands west to east: east
(255, 128, 255), north (128, 1, 255), south-west (38, 218, 255). Frames at drawn frames 14, 20, 29
and 44 (0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5 s). `evidence/shift.py`: per band (1 m inside its edges, above the sun's
highlight), the integer pixel shift maximising the normalised cross-correlation of frame 0 with

Read: the ripples move downstream along each band's own flow at a speed between map b's 0.5 m/s
and map a's 0.8 m/s, in both renderers, and the probe tells the pinned code from its two likeliest
wrong twins (lesson 34). At 0.5 s the comparison is ambiguous by construction: the two phases are
half a period apart, so map a's two copies of the pattern lie 0.8 m apart along the flow, and a 0.4 m
shift either way matches one of them (the north band picked the backward one). A still pair half a
second apart therefore cannot show the direction, and the 0.1 s pair is the one that measures it;
on screen at 60 frames per second each copy moves +0.013 m per frame, downstream. The probe took
20 s (Compatibility) and 35 s (Forward+). The two Forward+ frames 0.5 s apart, as the session asked,
are `shots/v0.4.0-03-bands-t00.jpg` and `shots/v0.4.0-03-bands-t30.jpg` (640 × 360): they show the
