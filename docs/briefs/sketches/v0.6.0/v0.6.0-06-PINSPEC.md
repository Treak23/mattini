## Open questions for the session

1. **The fixture's home.** Design §5 says the surroundings fixture is "given a colour input". I gave
   the colour its own fixture (`tests/shared/farcolour_fixture/`): the shared one's 15 × 18 km box
   at its 1000 m sample would need 270 photo cells (a 270-tile lock), and its random heights cannot
   be shaped into the band cases without changing v0.6.0-04's pinned bytes. Option kept: a separate
   fixture (easy to undo: delete a folder). Other: re-cut v0.6.0-04's fixture with a colour input.
2. **v0.6.0-08's `fixture_colour.jpg`** (a designed gradient, filled_fraction 0.2 by hand) is not
   what any step makes from the shared fixture. v0.6.0-07 writes the shared fixture's manifest from
   the steps: either it keeps v0.6.0-08's placeholder as game-only design data (the block then not
   reproducible by `manifest`), or a colour input is added to the shared fixture and v0.6.0-08's
   JPEG, manifest and GUT checks are re-cut. Not settled here; nothing of v0.6.0-08's changed.
3. **`colour` optional or required** in `check_surroundings_config`. Kept optional (v0.6.0-04's
   fixture has none; the far colour step refuses an area without it). Other: required, which needs
   question 1's other option first.
4. **The band mean's rounding.** Design: "the integer mean colour". Taken: half-up `(total + n // 2)
   // n`, the rounding of `(sum + 12) // 25`, over the covered 10 m pixels (not their source pixels).
   Others: floor; half-to-even; over the source pixels. The fixture tells all four apart.
5. **The description's shape.** The step writes `surroundings/<area>_colour.json` with v0.6.0-08's
   nine `colour_` keys exactly, so v0.6.0-07's block is the two descriptions side by side. It has no
   `area`, `world_sha256` or entry hash of its own (the heights' carry the first two; the colour's
   inputs hash lives only in `config/surroundings.json`). Other: a self-standing description with
   those keys, which v0.6.0-07 would then have to drop.
6. **The cell's fit check** (cell = regio resolution = 5 × far resolution, dividing the box and
   1000) is the session's, not the design's: it turns a datasets edit into a refusal instead of
   wrong pixels.
7. **The refusal when the GDAL decode differs** names rasterio's and GDAL's versions (H20 measured
   three decoders agreeing on the 10 cm tiles; the 2 m tiles were not measured across decoders).
8. **The look of the fill** (above): the km-stepped edge between photo and fill stays visible in a
   downscaled view. Design §2 expected the fill to remove "the probe's stepped edge"; it removes the
   grey ramp but not the step. A question for the owner's PC, not for this brief.
