## Open questions for the session

1. **How the fit learns the margin and spacing** (M4-01 left it to the builder: "a keyword
   argument, or the reader"). The pins take the reader inside `fit_problem` (decision 1):
   `fit_problem(lock, datasets, world, area, "swissaltiregio")` keeps the five-argument call
   every caller makes (the steps and `manifest` in v0.6.0-07 will ask it too), and the reader
   is read at call time. Option B: a keyword `margin_m`/`spacing_m` on `fit_problem`, passed by
   `download_regio` and later by the step; then `test_v060_02_download_regio.py`'s two fit
   tests change. The pinned choice is easier to undo (B only adds a parameter), and v0.6.0-07
   ("one home for the tile rule and readers") may move the reader onto `surroundings.py`
   anyway; then it re-pins `test_v060_01_regio_settings.py` and these two tests' patch point.
2. **The republish refusal's step** names `config/surroundings.json` alone. When v0.6.0-06 adds
   the colour block, the colour's `computed_for` also records the regio entry (design §5,
   `lock.entries_sha256` of both): the sentence stays right (one file holds both), but if the
   session wants the colour named, v0.6.0-05/06 re-pin the word-for-word test.
3. **`regio` among `__main__.DATASETS`** (decision 4) makes `lock regio` and `download regio`
   one table entry, and the run's count line `30 tiles verified`. v0.6.0-05 adds `farphoto`
   the same way; its absent cells (design §2: recorded in the lock as absent) will need a
   count line that says how many were absent, which this table's one unit does not express.
4. **The datetime refusal at download** reuses `published_regio`'s text, which ends "nothing
   was written" (v0.6.0-01's throwaway wording, unpinned); at download "nothing was fetched"
   would read better. Neither pin compares that tail; the builder's choice, or the session pins
   one word in v0.6.0-07.
5. **Gaps the prover should look at** (brief decision 8): provenance.json's removal before the
   first change on disk has no regio pin (shared code); the `.part`, retry and keep logic is
   shared (`_fetch_into`, `_fetch_verified`): a builder who writes a regio-only copy of them
   (forbidden by the brief) would be pinned only by the cases above.
6. **`test_m4_01_download_regio.py`'s whole-body case** swaps the served bytes back after the
   first GET by patching `download.urllib_fetch_file`, which `__main__._download` looks up at
   run time (as on `d56c21a`). A builder who binds the fetcher at import time breaks that test
   (not the behaviour). Option B: a `("bytes", …)` step in `local_swisstopo.py`'s `script`,
   which is v0.5.0-10's and M3-03's shared helper, re-pinned in their briefs: heavier, left out.
