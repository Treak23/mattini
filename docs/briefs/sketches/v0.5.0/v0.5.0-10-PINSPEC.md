## Open questions and notes for the session

1. **brain.md H9's sentence** "Python 3.13's urllib ends a body cut short before its
   `Content-Length` without an error; only the checksum catches it" stays true of urllib, but after
   this batch `urllib_fetch_file` catches it by the Content-Length. Recommendation: the session
   rewords H9 after the batch lands (Decision 8 keeps it out of the builder's Scope).
2. **The hint follows the last attempt only** (the pin author's reading of the triage's "when the
   last problem was a cut"): a 503 then a cut gets it, a cut then a 503 does not. A 503 may heal on
   a rerun too; the hint is kept to cuts as the triage proposed. Recommendation: keep.
3. **One new file, not re-pins** (Decision 7): the triage proposed cases in
   `test_surfaces_config.py` and a repeat in `test_roofs_step.py`; the pin author put all four
   items in one new file, so no re-pinned copy can collide with another brief's. Recommendation:
   keep.
4. **The pin does not name the exception class** (`pytest.raises(download.FETCH_ERRORS)`): the
   builder may take another shape (an `http.client.IncompleteRead`, which is an `HTTPException`,
   would need its text to hold `cut at N of M bytes`). Recommendation: keep it free.
5. **Row 8 is not a gap** (an existing test catches it); it stays as a row because the pin's three
   `[infinite-…]` cases are the swissimage and swisssurface3d sides of it.
6. **Rows 1, 7, 9, 10, 11 "survive the existing tests"** was measured on the 1489 related tests, not
   on the whole suite (2669): the brief's does-NOT-prove line says so.
7. Lane A runs after v0.5.0-07: `download.py` is touched by no other brief of 01 to 06; the session
   checks 07 to 09 when it writes them.
