## Goal

Distant forest in the shade no longer draws near-black: the sky rig turns on a light haze, Godot's
exponential fog coloured by the sky's own radiance (aerial perspective), so ground 2 km away is
about 10 % the sky's colour, the colour follows the moving sun and goes dark at night with no
per-frame code, and the sky itself is not fogged. The density is one named design constant,
`SkyRig.HAZE_DENSITY`, measured by you in software Forward+ and recorded. The gate's render checks
keep their numbers digit for digit: the two that draw the main scene switch the haze off, as they
already switch off ambient light. The owner gets software Forward+ frames of the start view and
the shaded slope at four hours, with and without the haze, to judge the look on.

Production code is `main` at the head you start from (rehearsed on the integration rehearsal's tree:
`main` at `d522abf` with v0.5.0-01 to -06's pins and throwaways, `8579310`). The pin author
rehearsed this brief there (2026-10-30): the pins red, green on a throwaway, the full gate, all 15
mutation rows, the density probe and every evidence frame on the real village. The pins were then
re-cut on `origin/main` at `b253cdd` (v0.5.0-01 to -06 landed by their builders; the checker's
fixes applied, rows 16 to 21 added) and rehearsed there again: red, green, all 21 rows both ways and
the full gate. Every count and RENDER line in the gates below is the re-cut's; the probe's shares
and the evidence table (decisions 3 and 7) are the first rehearsal's, marked "rehearsal". The pin author's record is
`docs/briefs/sketches/v0.5.0/v0.5.0-07-PINSPEC.md`; you need from it only the two scripts named
below.

## Decisions already made

"(pin author; accepted by the session 2026-10-30)" marks a choice the pin author made while
rehearsing.

1. **The haze is set once, in `SkyRig.create`** (the owner's V2, the go's question A; the triage's
   reading of 2026-10-29), after the tone map, on the environment it is handed:
   - `fog_enabled = true`;
   - `fog_mode = Environment.FOG_MODE_EXPONENTIAL` (exponential with distance, not depth fog);
   - `fog_density = HAZE_DENSITY`;
   - `fog_aerial_perspective = 1.0`: the fog's colour is the sky's radiance in that direction, so
     it follows the moving sun and goes dark at night;
   - `fog_sky_affect = 0.0`: the sky itself is not fogged.
   Every other fog property keeps Godot's default (`fog_height_density` 0: no height fog, so the
   density alone sets the haze; `fog_sun_scatter` 0; volumetric fog off; `fog_light_color` and
   `fog_light_energy` are not used at aerial perspective 1.0). The pins check the first three
   after `create` and after every `apply`: height density and sun scatter to within 1e-9, volumetric
   fog off. `create` sets the five whatever the environment held (the
   pins hand it the wrong value for each). `apply`, called every frame, touches none of them: no
   per-frame code.
2. **`HAZE_DENSITY` is a named design constant beside the others**, per metre, a `float`, with a doc
   comment that says what it is and what you measured (Hard Rule 2: the comment claims only the
   measurement you made). Start from 5.27e-5 (= -ln(0.9) / 2000 m, the record's guess). The pins
   hold it between 2e-5 and 1.5e-4 (about 4 % to 26 % at 2 km) and that the environment reads it,
   not its exact value.
3. **You measure the density** (gate M below) with the pin author's probe,
   `docs/briefs/sketches/v0.5.0/v0.5.0-07-probe.gd`: a black unshaded wall facing the camera at a
   distance D, white fog at aerial perspective 0, black background, linear tone map, MSAA off, in
   software Forward+; the centre pixel's linear value is the share Godot fogs at D. Keep
   `HAZE_DENSITY` if the share at 2000 m lies in 0.09 to 0.11; otherwise set it to
   `-ln(1 - 0.10) / 2000 * (0.10 / your share at the start value)` and measure again (one round
   is expected to be none: rehearsal, the share at 2000 m 0.0999, sRGB 89, at 1000 m 0.0513, at
   4000 m 0.1912, against 1 - exp(-density * D) 0.1000, 0.0513, 0.1901; Compatibility the same).
   Record the shares in Findings and in the constant's comment.
4. **The render checks switch the haze off** (pin author; accepted by the session 2026-10-30; the
   plan's seam 7). The rig's haze reaches every view of `main.tscn`, so the gate's
   `render_photo.gd` (both views) and `render_trees.gd` set `environment.fog_enabled = false` on
   the main scene's environment before their first capture, beside their other overrides: the fog
   adds the sky's colour to every pixel, and their numbers are ratios and correlations of the data.
   Rehearsal, with the haze and without the overrides: `RENDER photo` mirrored scans 0.0467 and
