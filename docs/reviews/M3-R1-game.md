### M3-R1-G-01 · P1 · The trees' flat normals point away from the viewer: every tree is lit from the wrong side, in both renderers

- **Where:** game/shaders/tree_conifer.gdshader:75 (`NORMAL = normalize(cross(dFdx(VERTEX), dFdy(VERTEX)));`). For contrast game/shaders/terrain_photo.gdshader:358 (Terrain3D's own flat normal: `cross(dFdyCoarse(VERTEX),dFdxCoarse(VERTEX))`, the other order). No test reads the line (game/tests/test_m3_11_tree_conifer.gd, test_tree_loader*.gd).
- **Claim:** In Godot 4.6.3 the face normal built from screen derivatives is `cross(dFdy(VERTEX), dFdx(VERTEX))`; the tree shader has the opposite order, so the `NORMAL` it writes points into the tree, away from the camera, and the sun lights every facet from its inside. `tree_conifer.gdshader`'s header says "flat normals from screen-space derivatives", and the plan's wave 2 record says "the conifers read near black there" of the Compatibility shots and names the owner's Forward+ as the check; the inversion is the same in Forward+.
