# v0.5.0 whole-range review: coordinates and determinism (R1)

**Lens:** Hard Rules 4 and 5, tier 1.
**Range:** `b5afe27..fe3cc40` on origin/main.

I traced the capture mask's placement through every writer: `shadows.py` → `shadows/<area>.json` → the manifest block → `CaptureShadowGrid` → `TerrainLoader._set_capture_shadow` → `terrain_photo.gdshader`. I also covered the flow check's axes, any second home for the origin or the axes, 32-bit LV95 arithmetic, outputs that could differ by machine, day or run, and how the release workflow reproduces design's bytes.

I made no changes to the repository. My worktree and scratch XDG folder are removed.

## Verdict

There is no P0 or P1 in this lens. The mask's placement is right end to end and has a single writer at each stage. The pipeline's sun and axes check out against an independent recomputation. There are four findings (one P2, three P3), and I confirm K3 with a regrade.

## What I checked and found correct

- **The mask grid, pipeline side.** The grid is aligned to the area's north-west corner: rows run north to south (`trees._place_tile` puts row 0 at n_max), columns west to east.
  - The march steps `dx = k sin A` east and `dy = -(k cos A)` rows south.
  - Own shadow uses `gy = dh/dN`, row above minus row below.
  - Sun vector is `(cos e sin A, cos e cos A, sin e)` in (east, north, up).
  - `build_shadows` refuses any world whose axes are not the owner's, so `shadows.py` applies no axis mapping. It writes LV95 only.
- **Mutations on `shadows.py`.** I ran three on top of brief 01's 19 rows:
  - own shadow reversed north-south: 82 failures;
  - the sun's east component mirrored in own shadow: 48 failures;
  - the built block's box read as n_max/n_min swapped: 12 failures.

  All three were caught.
- **The sun's numbers.** I recomputed the flight's sun with NOAA's algorithm: the mean over 11:25 to 11:55 UTC on 6 and 7 September is a true azimuth of 184.91° and an elevation of 50.09°, with a span of about 11.6°.
  - I recomputed the grid convergence with rasterio at the village centre (E 2624000, N 1096500): true north lies at grid azimuth −0.2258°.
  - So `config/shadows.json`'s 184.67° is right, the sign is right, and `test_m4_03_village_config.py` ties it to `config/sky.json`.
- **The game side.**
  - `CaptureShadowGrid._place` goes through `WorldConfig.to_local`, which subtracts in 64 bits.
  - The axes check uses `to_local` deltas.
  - Texel (c, r) is the half-open cell from the north-west corner, in both `value_at` and the GPU's texel centres.
  - The shader samples by its own `capture_shadow_rect`, not the photo's. Brief 04's mutation rows 1, 9 to 15 and 24 kill flips, shifts, transposition and photo-rect swaps.
  - The writers of `capture_shadow*` are `terrain_loader.gd:96-103` only, plus the test scripts.
  - `test_main_scene_refusals.gd:237-250` pins the main scene's hand-off.
- **No LV95 coordinates or axis literals in new production code.** Only the fixtures carry them. No new code reads the time, the host name or a random value.
- **The release workflow.**
  - Inputs are pinned by the lock, uv by `tools/pins.sh`, and the manifest pair is checked.
  - The manifest carries every generated file's SHA-256, and the game re-checks the mask's PNG and pixel hashes. So the dry run's matching pair proves the runner wrote design's generated bytes.
  - `release_pack.sh` fixes the entry times (SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH or commit time), uses `gzip -n`, `zip -X -D` with TZ=UTC, LC_ALL=C, and sorts SHA256SUMS.

## Findings

### R1-CD-01 [P2]: the render step does not see an east-west shift of the flow read either (extends K3)

**Where:** `tools/gate.sh:31-41`.

**Claim vs fact.** The header says "a flipped, shifted or swapped placement in a shader or a loader fails the gate", and makes only one exception for the flow: a north-south shift. An east-west shift also passes.

**What I ran.** `render_flow.gd` alone at fe3cc40, with line 503 of the shader changed to `texture(water_flow, (xz - vec2(s) - surfaces_rect.xy) / surfaces_rect.zw)`:

| Shift `s` | Where the flow is read | Result |
|---|---|---|
| (1, 0) | 1 m west | **PASS** |
| (−2, 0) | one whole 2 m flow cell east | **PASS** (east −4.8°, north −4.2°) |
| (2, 0) | 2 m west | FAIL |
| (0, −4) | 4 m south | FAIL |

The text census `WATER_RULE_LINES` (`test_m4_06_terrain_look.gd:143-145`) catches the shift. The render step does not.

**Harm.** The gate's words claim more than it compares (lesson 44). Nothing in the game is wrong today.

**Fix shape.** Reword the gate header and `render_flow.gd`'s does-not-prove list: the flow check does not see where the flow is read, either north-south or east-west (up to about one cell). Name the census and the loader test as what pins it. A test on the words, like `test_v0_5_0_09_gaps.py`.

### R1-CD-02 [P3]: the mask reader's test data cannot see a 32-bit origin subtraction (lesson 34, H5)

**Where:** `game/tests/test_m4_06_capture_shadow_grid.gd`.

**What I ran.** I replaced `to_local` in `shadow_grid.gd:284-286` with
`Vector2(e_min, n_max) - Vector2(origin_e, origin_n); z0 = -nw.y`. That is 32-bit, and a second home for z = −n. It survived:
- `test_m4_06_capture_shadow_grid.gd` 16/16
- `test_v0_5_0_03_gaps.gd` 9/9
- `test_main_scene_data.gd` 5/5
- `test_scene_placement.gd` 7/7

**Why it survives.** Every origin, box and texel centre in the fixtures is exact in float32. The only H5 trap point in `game/tests` is in `test_world_config_round_trips.gd`.

**Harm.** None today: the village's values are whole metres. With a future origin that float32 cannot hold, a regression would move the lift by up to 0.12 m with no test failing.

**Fix shape.** One test case with an origin such as E 2650012.13, asserting x0 and z0 to 1e-3 m. The earlier grid readers probably share this weakness; I did not check them.

### R1-CD-03 [P3]: render_lift.gd's header is stale

**Where:** `game/tests/render/render_lift.gd:7-8`.

The header says "(the main scene does not read the mask before brief M4-10)". Since v0.5.0-05, `main.gd` reads the mask and hands it to the terrain, and `test_main_scene_refusals.gd:237-250` pins that. This is a Hard Rule 2 comment problem.

**Fix shape.** Re-pin the header's words.

### R1-CD-04 [P3]: the workflow does not pin the interpreter whose zlib the PNG hashes depend on

**Where:** `.github/workflows/release.yml:81-87`.

**Evidence.**
- The dry run's log (job 123922918366) says: "Downloading cpython-3.13.14 … Using CPython 3.13.14", i.e. uv's managed build.
- This container's pipeline runs the system Python: `3.13.17 1.3 /usr`.
- `.python-version` says only `3.13`, and the workflow sets no `UV_PYTHON_PREFERENCE`. Yet `shadows.py:530-535` itself advises `only-managed`.

The two interpreters happened to agree. The runner got the managed build only because ubuntu-24.04 has no system python3.13.

**Harm.** No wrong bytes can ship: the steps refuse another deflate. But a future runner image could swap the interpreter without notice and make a release run fail on release day.

**Fix shape.** Set `UV_PYTHON_PREFERENCE: only-managed`, pin 3.13.14, and log the interpreter and its zlib version once.

## Known items: confirmed and graded

- **K3: confirmed.**
  - `render_flow.gd:41-46`'s does-not-prove list does not mention the north-south blind spot.
  - Every band texel has B = 255 (`render_flow.gd:201`), so dropping `* t.b` changes nothing in the render.
  - The data-blindness part is covered at the source level by the census: `test_m4_06_terrain_look.gd:145` pins `* t.b`, and lines 149-160 pin ripple map b. So that part harms only the render check's own claims. I would grade it P3; the session's P2 for the words stands.
  - R1-CD-01 extends K3 to east-west shifts.
- **The residue the batch log lists:** the flow check's own copy of the axes, RENDER lift's comparators reading 0.0, and the world guard's "origin differs" wording. The whole range does not make any of these worse.

## What this review does NOT prove

- That the mask lies on the photo's real shadows, or that anything looks right on a GPU. I ran single render scripts only, Compatibility on llvmpipe.
- That fe3cc40 reproduces the manifest pair on a runner. The dry run is of b7df28c. Between b7df28c and fe3cc40, the only changes in data-affecting files are messages in `surfaces.py` and the cut-transfer check in `download.py`.
- That the pck files or the archives are byte-reproducible across machines.
- That another libm writes the same mask. The pixel hash refuses one that does not.
- The earlier readers' H5 test data.
- K1, K2, K4 and K5, which are outside this lens.
- The full gate. I ran single files and named mutations only.
