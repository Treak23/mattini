### Arithmetic verified by reading
- **Square membership.** `column = (2(x−e_min)+s)//2s` and `row = (2(n_max−y)+s−1)//2s` are exactly the half-open square [E−s/2, E+s/2) × (N−s/2... N+s/2]. The west and south edges belong to the vertex. Floor division is correct for negative values.
- **Mean.** An int64 sum of whole centimetres is divided once in float64 by `100.0·n`, the offset is subtracted, and the result is cast once to float32. The NaN is written by bit pattern.
- **Colour.** The block mean is `(sum+12)//25` in int64. The band is `cents // (100·band_m)`, a floor also below 0 m. The band mean is `(total+n//2)//n`. `_donor_band` takes the nearest band below, else the nearest above.
- **Registration.**
  - Pixel (r, c) is centred at (e_min+5+10c, n_max−5−10r), and the tile placement offsets are whole cells.
  - Image row 0 is north, and v = r/(rows−1) maps to it.
  - u = c/(columns−1) places a vertex exactly on a texel boundary, which is the right registration of 50 m vertices on 10 m pixels.
- **Meshing.**
  - Square winding, the skirt's four winding and facing cases, and the vertex normals (central differences, one-sided at the box edge and next to NaN) are consistent with Godot's clockwise-front rule.
  - The skirt's wall closes at the cut's corners.
  - Chunk lattices share vertices and normals. The chunk count is 11×11 = 121.
- **Terrain seam.** The near terrain is drawn exactly to the box edge: `terrain_loader.gd` holes every non-grid vertex, and the far grid keeps the edge vertices. Plan positions coincide. There is no overlap and no gap in plan.

## Findings (details in the data)

- **F1, P1 (K1).** The free-fly camera is kept above the near terrain only, so it can fly into the far mountains and see them from below. Likelihood is high, because flying out toward the mountains is the point of 0.6.0.
  - The ground-keeping callback is `_ground_height`, which returns `_terrain.data.get_height(...)`. It is wired only in `_show_terrain`.
  - The repo's own `brain.md:143` and `test_terrain_loader*.gd` pin that `get_height()` is NaN outside the regions and on holes.
  - The loader holes everything outside the 2×3 km grid.
  - `_keep_above_ground` does nothing for a non-finite ground. The camera runs at up to 1000 m/s, or 5000 m/s fast. It has no physics: collision is M6, and SPEC §10 records "flies freely where there is no ground".
  - Nothing in design §4 or the briefs accepts flying under the far mesh. The far material is `CULL_BACK`. A camera under the surface sees the mountain's underside as nothing.
  - The edge case is real but needs a measurement I could not make (unverified magnitude).
    - The edge vertex is the mean of a 50 m square, half of it inside the box.
    - On a curved flank that differs from the 0.5 m surface by about k·104 m for curvature k, roughly 0.5 to 2 m, the same order as `clearance_m` = 1.5 (not 2).
    - So the far edge can stand above a camera kept 1.5 m over the near terrain. At the cut it shows as the skirt wall.
    - Crossing the edge puts the camera under the far surface.
  - When the terrain is refused but the far terrain loads, `height_at` is never set, and the camera is free everywhere, far mesh included.
  - **Smallest fix.**
    - Add `SurroundingsGrid.ground_height(x, z)`. It returns the height on the mesh's own two triangles, using the NE–SW diagonal the loader builds, and NaN in the cut, over an undrawn square, or outside the box.
    - In `main.gd`, make `_ground_height` try the terrain first and then the far grid.
    - Set `_camera.height_at` in `_load_world` when either exists.
    - `free_fly_camera.gd` needs no change.
    - Pin it with a test that a camera placed under the far mesh outside the box is lifted. Bilinear interpolation would not do: it can sit metres below the triangles.
- **F2, P2 (K3).** STAC item reads have no retry, so one connection reset fails the whole `download farphoto`, and so the Release run.
  - `urllib_fetch_json` makes one attempt. `_published_asset` converts any failure into a DownloadError. `_in_parallel` raises it.
  - `download farphoto` re-reads all 1806 items on every run, the 586 known-absent cells (HTTP 404) included. The 1220 file fetches already retry once (`ATTEMPTS`), but the item reads do not.
  - In `release.yml` the far downloads come last, after `download tlm` and `download surface`. The workflow has no cache and no retry, so a reset in `download farphoto` discards the whole job.
  - Likelihood is unverified. The briefs record three aborts (one `lock farphoto`, two `download farphoto`) with "Remote end closed" and "Connection reset by peer". The v0.6.0-06 run blamed the agent proxy ("server_draining"), which a GitHub runner does not have. The briefs give no run count, so I cannot estimate a rate. I would not call a failed build unlikely.
  - A retry is safe for determinism.
    - A GET of the same URL is idempotent.
    - The result is compared with the lock before any file is fetched, and the files are checked against SHA-256.
    - `lock farphoto` writes only after all reads succeed.
  - The retry must not apply to HTTP 404 (it means "absent") or to other 4xx answers.
  - **Fix shape.** One retry inside `urllib_fetch_json` for non-4xx errors. Tests inject `fetch_json`, so their call counts do not move.
- **F3, P3 (K2).** Seven sites in six files check a digest as "length 64 and `is_valid_hex_number()`", and that function accepts a leading `+` or `-`. The sites are `tree_chunks.gd:319`, `building_chunks.gd:190`, `surface_grid.gd:281` and `:324`, `terrain_grid.gd:212`, `shadow_grid.gd:372` and `photo_grid.gd:200`.
  - A signed file digest is still refused. `found != digest.to_lower()` compares with the file's true SHA-256, so the refusal says "has SHA-256 X, not Y" instead of "must be 64 hex digits".
  - A digest that is never compared is accepted, and the game runs. These are `photo.pixels_sha256`, `shadows.source.entry_sha256` and the trees' `source.tiles[i].sha256`.
  - No owner-visible effect. The refusal message is wrong, and a shape check is weaker than its text.
  - **Fix shape.** Share `SurroundingsGrid._is_digest` or a helper that checks every character.
- **F4, P3 (K4).** `surroundings.py:600` and `farcolour.py:404` handle a DownloadError that cannot occur.
  - Both callers validate the same inputs first (`_regio_config`, `_far_config`, the area in `world.areas`). `edition` is then present, so `edition_of` cannot raise. `surroundings_margin_spacing` finds the area from `configured_area`, and `world.area` cannot fail.
  - `test_v060_07_gaps.py:435-460` says "no datasets config reaches the conversion". It gets there only by replacing `download.regio_tiles` or `download.farphoto_cells` with a stand-in that raises.
  - Harmless dead code with a gate that proves only the stand-in. Delete the handlers and their two tests, or keep them as defence and say so.
  - The same shape in `manifest._inputs_check` is reachable: `datasets` is not validated beforehand there.
- **F5, P3 (words).**
  - `main.gd:44` says the far plane "reaches every corner of the far terrain". The test and brief prove it only from inside the area box. The window's diagonal is about 60 km, so from its other corner the far half is clipped, though fog hides about 88 % at 40 km.
  - `FAR_COLOUR_NOTE` (`main.gd:138-140`) hard-codes "100 m of altitude". `band_m` is design data and not in the manifest block, so the note would lie if design changed the band.

## Things checked and found correct (no finding)

- **Reader and loader.**
  - The NaN-layout check in the game and the pipeline use the same strict-inside rule.
  - The block key sets and the colour dimension check against the box agree.
  - The reader refuses a null or stale block in the same words as its siblings.
  - The texture size guard (16384) is in place.
  - The skirt is double-sided with shadows off, as design §7 amends.
- **Refusal paths.**
  - Every step refusal names what is wrong, and the `download`, `lock` or design step that fixes it, in the order the docstring states. The one wording gap is that `surroundings` prints a "reading <tile>" line before a missing tile file's refusal, while the docstring says "before any progress line"; that is cosmetic.
  - Nothing is written before the SHA-256 comparisons pass (`surroundings`: heights; `farcolour`: pixels, then fraction, then JPEG).
  - Files go through `.part` and rename, and `.part` leftovers are cleared after a success. The previous output is intact after a refusal.
  - A crash between the two `.f32` and `.json` renames leaves a manifest block that the sha comparison refuses.
- **Parallel paths.**
  - Output order and `provenance.json` order do not depend on completion order.
  - Provenance is removed before the first change on disk and written only at the end of a completed run.
  - Ctrl-C waits for the running calls after cancelling the pending ones.
  - Only a non-refusal exception (a bug) at a higher index can hide a refusal at a lower one. That is not a finding.
- **Release.** `export.sh` ships the far files in `mattini.pck`. The largest is 5.9 MB, under the 10 MiB limit of step 4c. The `.import` is not shipped. The startup-line count is 8 plus the packs. The workflow order is `surroundings`, `farcolour`, then `shadows`, as `python -m mattini` orders its steps.
- **Seam P3, ruled by the session.** Running `farcolour` before `surroundings` writes the colour files, then ends in an error because the manifest write is refused until `surroundings` runs.
