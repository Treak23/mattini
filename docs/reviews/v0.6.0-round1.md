session's model (Sonnet 5.5 for this release: the seams between batches, correctness, what the owner sees, coordinates and

**Result: no P0, one P1, one P2.** The P1 is the headline feature's: the free-fly camera is kept above the near terrain only, so
leaving the 2 km x 3 km area box (seconds at 30 m/s, 1.2 km to the box's west edge on the start bearing) the owner flies into the
far mountainside and sees the ground vanish (the far mesh is back-face culled). It had been seen in v0.6.0-09's first review round,
lost with a spend limit and not carried by its final report; eight skeptics of eight upheld P1. The P2 is the STAC item reads' missing
retry. Everything else is P3. The coordinates and determinism lens found no second home for the origin, no 32-bit subtraction, no
mirrored or shifted placement and nothing in the pipeline's bytes that could differ by machine or run.

WORKFLOW's P0-and-P1 rule (the 0.5.0 round did the same), the words that claim what the code does not do, and the Release notes
(lesson 2's twin of 0.5.0's P1: the notes must carry the credits and placeholders the game shows). The session's own words (SPEC,
