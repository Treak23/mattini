### P3-2 Ripple map b jumps once an hour

- Where: `game/shaders/terrain_photo.gdshader:102-103, 455`; `game/project.godot` sets no
  `rendering/limits/time/time_rollover_secs` (`grep rollover` prints nothing), so TIME wraps at
  Godot's default 3600 s.
- Claim: at the wrap map a moves 3600 × (0.48, 0.64) / 2 = (864, 1152) tiles, whole numbers, so
  it is seamless; map b moves 3600 × (0.48, 0.64) / 3.4 = (508.235, 677.647) tiles, so its
  pattern jumps 0.8 m east-west and 2.2 m north-south in one frame, every hour of play. 0.3.0's
  maps had the same kind of jump (0.23 × 3600 / 10 = 82.8); this release chose new numbers and
  did not avoid it.
- Harm: once an hour, half the river's ripples snap to a new place. Small, easily missed, and
  exactly the "looks weird" kind of thing the owner reported on the water.
- Fix: a scale for b that divides 3600 × 0.48 and 3600 × 0.64 (3.2 m or 3.6 m give whole
  tiles), or wrap the offset in the shader with `mod(TIME * water_drift, tile × k)`; the census
  pin of `water_scale_b_m = 3.4` moves with it. A design call for 0.4.0, not a fix-batch item.

### P3-3 water_weight's cost is paid on every terrain pixel and never measured

- Where: `terrain_photo.gdshader:161-181` (36 `texelFetch` per call) called unconditionally at
  `:452`, before `if (w_water > 0.0)`; 0.3.0 read the water from `class_weights`' four fetches.
- Claim: every terrain fragment at every distance now reads 36 class texels at mip 0 of the
  10'000 × 15'000 class image; far away, neighbouring pixels land metres apart, so the fetches
  share little cache. Nobody measured it: the gate's render is a 20 m flat test world under
  llvmpipe.
- Harm: unknown, probably small on an RTX 3060 at 60 fps; but the look list asks the owner to
  compare the GPU load with 0.3.0's 94 %, and this cost is folded into that number unseen.
- Fix: none required now; say in the record that the river's shader reads 9× more class texels
  per pixel than 0.3.0's, so a higher-than-expected load at 60 fps has a named suspect. A later
  shape: an early out on a coarse water mask (a mip or a max-pooled copy), beyond 0.5 m of any
  water texel.

  display whose rate is not a multiple of 60.
