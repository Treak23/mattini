## Known items
- **K1 confirmed, P1.** `height_at` is NaN outside the near terrain. That is pinned by `test_terrain_loader.gd:104,115`, which asserts NaN for holes and for positions outside the regions. `free_fly_camera.gd:114` does nothing where the height is NaN.
  - The camera starts at the area box's centre, local (1000, -500), facing bearing 235 degrees. That is the direction of the Matterhorn, which is outside the box.
  - The box's west edge is 1.2 km away along that line: about 8 s at fast speed (150 m/s), about 40 s at 30 m/s.
  - Past the edge the camera has no floor. The chunks use `CULL_BACK` (`surroundings_loader.gd:38`), so from below the mountainside is culled and the owner looks through it.
  - Nothing tests the camera against the far terrain. The only camera test of 0.6.0 is the far plane.
- **K2 confirmed, P3.** There are seven sites in six files. A 64-character `+` or `-` plus 63 hex digits passes each of them.
- **K3 confirmed, P2.** There is no retry on a STAC read; I found one documented reset behind the agent proxy.
- **K4 confirmed, P3.** Both `except DownloadError` lines are unreachable by construction.

Details are in `known` and in the findings.

## First-run command experience
Mostly usable. Every refusal I read names the file, what is wrong and the command that fixes it: `download regio` for a missing download, `lock regio` or `lock farphoto` for a lock that does not fit, `surroundings` for a colour built without heights.

Gaps:
- A transient STAC failure ends a 1806-item read with no "run it again" hint and no progress output.
- `farcolour` run before `surroundings` does its roughly 80 s of work and only then fails at the manifest write. The message is accurate, and the panel and the reader's null message give the right order.
